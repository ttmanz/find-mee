Delete My Account

You can permanently delete your Find-Mee account and all associated data at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete from inside the app (instant)

  • Open Find-Mee and sign in
  • Go to Profile Settings
  • Scroll down and tap "Delete my account"
  • Confirm — your account is erased immediately

If you can no longer access the app, you can request deletion by email instead. We will process your request within 30 days.

What will be deleted

  • Your profile and account information
  • Your posts, stories, and user-generated content
  • Your messages and chat history
  • Your events and Market listings
  • Your location history and check-ins
  • All personal data associated with your account
Request Account Deletion

This opens an email to support@find-mee.com with a pre-filled deletion request.
